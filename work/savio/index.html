How it works

  1. It picks its moment. Not a popup at checkout, when you have already decided. It steps in when your salary lands, when a windfall arrives, and a few days after you bought something. Quiet moments, when you can actually think.
  2. The AI does the explaining, grounded in your own figures. Code produces the numbers. The AI turns them into something you would act on, and it can only draw on what is really yours: your commitments, your safety net, what is left this month. That is the harder half of the job. A correct number nobody reads changes nothing.
  3. It quotes your own rule back to you. Not “this is over budget”, but “over the ₹3,000 impulse-wait limit you set”. A rule you wrote yourself is a rule you will accept.

The calls I made

  • Every number gets traced. The AI is good at explaining; it is not the place to source a figure. So each number it writes is checked back against your real data before you see it, and anything that can't be traced gets replaced with a note telling you to check.
  • Your safety net is the floor. Savio flags anything that would eat into the cash you said you want to keep, or into what you have already promised elsewhere. Green, amber or red, with the reason.
