Not linked from anywhere and not indexed. Nothing here is on the live site.

  • When the next section arrives, its heading should push the last one out rather than overlapping it.
  • Phone first. This is where knowing which section you are in actually helps.
  • Flip the theme — the pinned heading has an opaque background and it has to match its own section.
