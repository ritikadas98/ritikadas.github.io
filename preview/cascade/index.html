About

The short version. The long version is the rest of the page.

I became a PM inside enterprise SaaS. Long cycles, big rooms, every client twenty years my senior.

It taught me to own the gap between what gets promised and what ships. But nobody in enterprise picks the software. Someone buys it and everyone else complies. Nothing I shipped was ever anyone’s choice.

Consumer is the opposite, and it is the part I actually want. Millions of people who can leave. A test that answers you in a week instead of a quarter. Design and psychology doing the real work. Why someone abandons a form, why they trust one number and not another, why they’d rather guess than ask.

So I started building it myself. Three products in, I’m not going back.
